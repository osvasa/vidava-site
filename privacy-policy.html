Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

What VIDAVA Never Collects

Your full card number, security code, expiration date and billing address never enter our systems. Your bank username and password never enter our systems; when you choose to connect a bank, Plaid handles the login and VIDAVA never sees it.

VIDAVA, Inc. makes the VIDAVA app, the VIDAVA browser extension and the VIDAVA online checkout. This policy applies to all three.

What We Collect

  • Your email address, to sign you in with a one time code.
  • The cards you add: the card's name as the bank names it, the bank, the last four digits, and the rewards, fees and terms of that card product.
  • If you connect a bank through Plaid: the card's balance, credit limit, interest rate, payment due date and, where supported, your transactions on that card. Plaid, Inc. provides the connection; its end user privacy policy applies to how Plaid handles your bank login.
  • When you pay with a VIDAVA recommendation: the store, the purchase category and the amount, so we can show what you earned and keep your rewards caps accurate.
  • The choices you make in the app, such as the category you picked for a card.

How We Use Your Data

For one purpose: to pick the best of your cards for each purchase and show you why. Card data and bank data are stored so they sync across your devices and so recommendations work without delay. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

Who We Share It With

  • Plaid, Inc., to connect your bank when you choose to.
  • Supabase, Inc., which hosts our database with encryption at rest and in transit.
  • Anthropic, PBC, whose Claude API we use to read checkout pages and card terms. We do not send Anthropic your name, email, balances or transactions, and Anthropic does not use the data to train models.

VIDAVA does not sell, rent or share your personal information for marketing. We never share your data in a form that identifies you.

Security

Only the account holder can read their rows in our database. Bank access tokens are held server side only. Every account that can reach our systems uses multi factor authentication.

Your Rights and Controls

  • Remove any card at any time in the app or extension.
  • Disconnect a bank at any time in Settings; we delete its tokens and data.
  • Delete your account at any time in Settings; we delete everything we hold about you, including the Plaid connections.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or any question at hello@vidava.app. We answer within 30 days.

Retention

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete a card, a bank connection or your account, that data is deleted from our systems. Logs that contain no personal data are kept for 30 days.

Cookies and Tracking

VIDAVA does not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers or third party tracking scripts. The extension reads a page only when it is a checkout.

Children

VIDAVA is not for anyone under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We post changes on this page with a new date. Material changes are also shown in the app.

Contact

VIDAVA, Inc. Questions about your privacy? Reach us at hello@vidava.app.
